Why coloring is so good for toddlers

Those 15 quiet minutes are building more than you might think.

Strong little hands

Gripping a crayon and staying near the lines builds the same muscles they'll use to write.

Colors & words

"Which color is the bow?" Coloring is a natural moment to learn colors, shapes and new words.

Calm & focus

One page, one crayon, one job. It helps busy little minds slow down, perfect before naps.

"I made this!"

Finishing a page gives a real sense of pride. Hang it on the fridge and watch them glow.
